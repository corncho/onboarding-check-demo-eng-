Two short check-ins in a new hire's first three months

We check in with every new hire at Day 30 and Day 90 to confirm we hired well, see how each person is settling in, and keep improving hiring and onboarding.

Who does what

Round 1 is a single step for each person. In Round 2 the work passes from the new hire to the hiring manager to the executive, and each person notifies the next one by email.

Round 1
Day 30
New hireAnswers the hiring quality survey. Done once submitted.
Hiring managerReviews each new hire. Done once submitted.
Round 2: the hand-off
1New hire

Survey and self-assessment

  1. Open My Tasks from the email
  2. Answer the survey and write the self-assessment
  3. Done once you notify your manager
EmailThe next step opens automatically
2Hiring manager

Self-assessment feedback

  1. Open the new hire's email
  2. Write feedback, then meet and sign
  3. Done once submitted

Rate 7 items and request approval

  1. Rate each item and submit
  2. Done once you notify the executive
No need to wait for the new hire's email. You can start right away.
EmailThe next step opens automatically
3Executive

Final approval

  1. Open the manager's email
  2. Review the ratings, add your score and comments, and sign
  3. Done once submitted

Good to know

Links close after the due date

Due dates appear in the email and in My Tasks, and turn red when close.

Submit once

Wait for the confirmation screen. To change an answer later, ask the Talent Development Team to reopen it with your earlier answers filled in.

The next step opens by itself

Keep My Tasks open and it refreshes every 30 seconds. If it loads slowly, wait up to a minute.

Use your own link, on any device

Open the link next to your name in the email. It works on a computer or a phone. On a phone, the notification email cannot be drafted automatically, so copy the text instead.
