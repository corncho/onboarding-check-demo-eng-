New Hire Onboarding CheckTalent Development Team

Two short check-ins in a new hire's first three months

We check in with every new hire at Day 30 and Day 90. The goal is to confirm we hired well, see how each person is settling in, and keep improving hiring and onboarding.

1. The process at a glance

Day 0Start dateNew hire joins
Day 30Round 1Hiring Quality Check
Day 90Round 2Onboarding Check
ROUND 1 | ABOUT 1 MONTH AFTER JOINING

Did we hire well?

A quick check on the hiring experience and the first month on the job.
New hireShort surveyabout 5 min
Hiring managerReviews the hireabout 10 min
ROUND 2 | ABOUT 3 MONTHS AFTER JOINING

How are they settling in?

A check on adaptation and performance, finished with an executive sign-off.
New hireSurvey and self-assessment5 to 10 min
Hiring managerFeedback, then ratings on 7 itemsabout 10 min
ExecutiveFinal approvalabout 5 min
