The Talent Development Team runs the New Hire Onboarding Check to make sure new hires are settling in well and to hear your feedback on the hiring and onboarding experience.

What is the New Hire Onboarding Check?
A short check-in at one month (Round 1) and three months (Round 2) after joining, to see how new hires are settling in and how the hiring process went. Open the link next to your name in the table below to see everything you need to do in one place. Just follow the steps.

Each step has a due date. Links close after it, so please check the dates and finish on time.

  • Time needed: about 5 minutes
  • Due date: Oct 16 (Fri)
  • You can also respond on your phone. (On a phone, the notification email cannot be drafted automatically, so copy the text instead.)
  • See the attached guide for details.
